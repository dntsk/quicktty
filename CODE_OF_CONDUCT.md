# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

QuickTTY contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- giving specific, actionable, and considerate feedback;
- respecting different experience levels and viewpoints;
- focusing disagreement on technical decisions rather than people;
- accepting correction and taking responsibility for mistakes;
- protecting other people's privacy and safety.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- any conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the QuickTTY project.

## Reporting and Enforcement

The project does not currently provide a private conduct-reporting channel. To report a problem, open a concise [GitHub issue](https://github.com/dntsk/quicktty/issues/new) without including private information, credentials, or other sensitive details.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants whose behavior is harmful or repeatedly disruptive. Enforcement decisions will consider context, severity, and whether the behavior continues after a clear request to stop.

Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is not acceptable.
